Our Story

Started on the Store Floor, Not in a Server Room

Northgrid began with a simple observation: most IT companies understood networks and hardware, but very few understood retail. Stores don’t stop for maintenance windows. Checkout lines don’t wait for a ticket to be picked up. We built our company around that reality — treating every store, warehouse, and office as a location where technology has to simply work.

Over the past decade, that focus has taken us from supporting single stores to managing technology across multi-location retail chains — spanning supermarkets, fashion retail, pharmacies, warehouses, and corporate offices. As retail has evolved with POS platforms, self-service kiosks, video analytics, and automation, our team has grown alongside it, adding the specialists needed to support each layer of the retail technology stack.

Today, we operate as an extension of our clients’ internal teams — monitoring networks overnight, deploying new stores on tight timelines, and answering the call when a POS terminal goes down on a Saturday afternoon. Our vision going forward is the same as it was on day one: to be the technology partner retailers can rely on, at every location, every day.
